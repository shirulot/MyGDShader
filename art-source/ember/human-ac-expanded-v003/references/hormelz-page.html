Free 8-Directional Melee Character

A downloadable asset pack

Download NowName your own price

An 8-directional animated sprite of a Melee Character. Perfect for HD2D / 2D3D pixel art-styles. You can use the up, down, left, and right sprite sheets as a standard 4-directional top-down character or even just the left and right animations for a sidescroller or platformer. Each animation has a .gif preview. 

Contains over 30 free animations! The asset license, which you can learn more about by clicking on the "More information" tab, is Creative Commons Zero(CC0)! This basically means you can use this assist in any way you like without attribution. No generative ai used.

If you make a game using this asset please share it with me in the comments below or on any social media. I'd love to see your creations!

*Each sprite sheet ends with a "_dir#" tag. They correspond to the following: 

  • dir1 = Down Left 
  • dir2 = Left
  • dir3 = Up Left
  • dir4 = Up
  • dir5 = Up Right
  • dir6 = Right
  • dir7 = Down Right
  • dir8 = Down

Canvas size: 126x132
